What We Track

This platform monitors SEC filings (10-Q, 10-K, 8-K) from major technology and infrastructure companies to extract signals about compute demand trends. We analyze statements about:

  • Demand Direction: Is compute demand increasing, decreasing, or staying flat?
  • Infrastructure Constraints: Power, chip availability, networking, datacenter capacity
  • Infrastructure Segments: AI training, AI inference, general compute
  • Market Dynamics: Pricing trends and time horizons

Methodology

What qualifies as a signal: We extract individual sentences from MD&A sections and business updates that explicitly mention compute infrastructure, capacity, or demand. Each sentence is analyzed independently.

Classification approach: Rule-based keyword matching maps text to structured labels. For example, "demand" + "increasing" = demand direction "up"; mentions of "power," "energy," or "electricity" = constraint type "power"; references to "AI," "GPU," or "accelerator" indicate AI-specific segments.

Confidence scoring: Confidence reflects signal specificity. High confidence (0.8-1.0) indicates multiple relevant keywords and clear directional language. Medium confidence (0.6-0.8) means partial matches or ambiguous phrasing. This is a heuristic measure, not a statistical model output.

Known limitations: SEC filings often use boilerplate language. Some signals may represent general datacenter trends rather than AI-specific capacity. Repeated risk factors across quarters may create duplicate signals. We filter risk factor sections but cannot eliminate all noise.
